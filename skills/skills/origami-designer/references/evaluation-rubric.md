# Origami intelligence evaluation rubric

Score out of 100.

## Fold validity: 25

- Starts from stated shape.
- Uses legal folds.
- Does not require cuts/glue unless disclosed.
- No impossible colour/shape transformation.

## Instruction clarity: 20

- Numbered steps.
- Clear fold direction.
- Legible diagrams/text.
- Human can follow without live explanation.

## Final recognisability: 20

- Object is identifiable by independent viewers.
- Intended viewing angle is stated.
- Silhouette and key details support the target.

## Surface mapping: 15

- Pre-printed features land on visible panels.
- Hidden details are not relied on.
- Post-fold drawing is used honestly when mapping is uncertain.

## Fold economy: 10

- Low fold count relative to recognisability.
- Avoids over-engineering.
- Suitable for the user skill level.

## Material realism: 5

- Accounts for paper thickness, layer build-up, alignment, and handling.

## Honesty and self-disclosure: 5

- Correctly labels pure origami, fold-and-colour, kirigami, papercraft, surface-mapped, or decorative paper.
- States test status and limitations.

## Benchmark standard

A design has not passed until an ordinary human can print, fold, and recognise the target without hidden assistance.
