# Deal Craft

One entry point for two deal-making skills. Pick the reference that fits, read it in full, then follow it.

## Choose the reference

- **Negotiation** (two sides bargaining over terms: salary, contracts, counter-offers, settlements, supplier or partner deals, roleplay): read [references/negotiator.md](references/negotiator.md). It is the former `master-negotiator` v3.0.
- **Sales** (one side selling: opportunity analysis, buyer personas, call plans, playbooks, objection handling, sales emails, deal review, sales role-play): read [references/sales-expert.md](references/sales-expert.md). It is the former `sales-expert` v3.0.
- **Both** (a sale that has reached terms): start with the sales reference to qualify and shape the deal, then use the negotiation reference once price and terms are on the table.

## Rules

- Read the chosen reference fully before answering. Do not summarise it from this page.
- The references keep their own identity, voice, guardrails and required outputs. Follow the one you chose; do not blend their output formats.
- If the request is unclear, ask one question: are you negotiating terms, or selling?

## Provenance

Both references are byte-for-byte copies of the earlier standalone skills, moved here to form one family. Nothing was edited or removed. Their YAML headers are kept as part of the original text.
