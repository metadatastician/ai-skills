How accessible this website is

This website is fully conformant with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines version 2.3 at Level AAA. It scores 100 out of 100 in automated accessibility audits, and is checked every night by our AccessGuard AI, so you can be confident every page meets the standard.

Our AI assistant can rewrite any page in Easy Read, British Sign Language video or another language on request, instantly and accurately.

Testing

This website was last tested on 1 November 2026 by AccessGuard AI, an automated tool. All issues it found have been fixed.
