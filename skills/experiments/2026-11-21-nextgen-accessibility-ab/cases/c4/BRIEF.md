# Case 4: reading settings panel on a library e-books service

A public library's e-book reading service has a "Reading settings" panel.
Signed-in readers can set text size, contrast, motion, font, line spacing,
reading level and a screen reader mode. The settings follow the reader across
devices. Pages are served through a third-party personalisation and delivery
service (Lumen Edge) that adapts the page before it reaches the reader.

Files: `settings.html` (the panel and its script), `integration.md` (how the
settings reach Lumen Edge).

Review them for accessibility.
