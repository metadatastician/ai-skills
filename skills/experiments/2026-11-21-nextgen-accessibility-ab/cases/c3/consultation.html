What we propose

We propose a bus lane on Riverside Road between the Mill Street junction and the station, operating 7am to 10am and 4pm to 7pm on weekdays. Outside those hours the lane would be open to all traffic.

Twelve on-street parking spaces on the north side would be removed. Four blue badge bays would be moved 40 metres east, to the new layby outside number 62.

Our traffic modelling suggests bus journeys could be up to 4 minutes quicker at peak times. Car journeys on Riverside Road may be up to 2 minutes slower.
