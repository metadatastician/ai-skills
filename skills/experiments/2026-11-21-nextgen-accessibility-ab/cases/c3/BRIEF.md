# Case 3: council consultation with "Simplify this page"

A city council runs an online consultation on a new bus lane. The consultation
page has a "Simplify this page" button, which loads a simpler version written
by an AI service on the council's server, and a "Read aloud" button. Responses
are collected by the form at the bottom of the page.

Files: `consultation.html` (the page and its script), `simplified.html` (what
the server returns for the simplified view), `server-notes.md` (the
developer's notes on the endpoints).

Review them for accessibility.
