# Case 6: official statistics release with AI summary and audio briefing

A national statistics office publishes a quarterly labour market bulletin. The
bulletin page shows an "At a glance" summary generated by an AI tool from the
release data, a chart with a text alternative and a full data table. An
"Audio briefing" button plays a short synthetic-voice briefing, whose script is
included.

Files: `bulletin.html` (the page), `audio-briefing.txt` (the audio script, as
generated).

Review them for accessibility.
