Main points

The estimated employment rate for people aged 16 to 64 was 74.1% in July to September 2026. This is 0.2 percentage points lower than the previous quarter. The change is within the sampling variability of the survey and is not statistically significant.

The estimated number of people in employment fell by 41,000 on the quarter. The 95% confidence interval for this change is plus or minus 96,000.

The unemployment rate was estimated at 4.4%, unchanged on the quarter.

Response rates to the Labour Force Survey remain low, and estimates should be treated with caution. See quality and methodology.
