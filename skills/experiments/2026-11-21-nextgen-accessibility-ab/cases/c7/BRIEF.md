# Case 7: recorded public lecture with captions, transcript and AI summary

A university publishes recordings of its public debates. Each recording page
has the video with edited captions, a full transcript and an "AI summary" for
readers who want the gist. The summary is made by the video platform.

Files: `lecture.html` (the page), `captions-excerpt.vtt` (part of the edited
captions file).

Review them for accessibility.
