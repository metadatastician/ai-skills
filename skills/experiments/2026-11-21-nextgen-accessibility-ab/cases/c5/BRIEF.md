# Case 5: product page tuned for AI shopping assistants

An online kitchenware retailer has rebuilt its product pages to be "AI agent
ready", so that shopping assistants and AI browsers can read and buy from them
reliably. The team added rich ARIA, structured data and a hidden facts block,
and the page refreshes the price live from stock.

Files: `product.html` (the page and its script).

Review it for accessibility.
