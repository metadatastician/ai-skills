# Case 1: local news article

A regional news site, the Wendham Courier, publishes this article. The site
shows an automatically generated "Key points" box at the top of every article
and offers "Listen to this article", which reads the page aloud with a
synthetic voice. The same key points are used as the article's meta
description and are sent to news aggregators.

Files: `article.html` (the page as served, including the key points box).

Review it for accessibility.
