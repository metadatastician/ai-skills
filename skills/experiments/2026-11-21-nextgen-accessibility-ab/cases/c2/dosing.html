Liquid paracetamol for children: how much to give

Page last reviewed: 3 November 2026. Next review due: November 2027.

Easy Read version

Before you start

  • Use the 120mg in 5ml liquid (infant suspension) for children under 6.
  • Always use the syringe or spoon that comes with the medicine.
  • If your baby is under 2 months old, speak to a doctor or pharmacist before giving paracetamol, unless it is after vaccinations.

How much to give

Dose of 120mg/5ml liquid paracetamol by age
AgeDoseHow often
2 to 3 months (after vaccinations)2.5mlOne dose, then a second dose after 4 to 6 hours if needed. No more than 2 doses.
3 to 6 months2.5mlUp to 4 times in 24 hours
6 to 24 months5mlUp to 4 times in 24 hours
2 to 4 years7.5mlUp to 4 times in 24 hours
4 to 6 years10mlUp to 4 times in 24 hours

Leave at least 4 hours between doses. Do not give more than 4 doses in 24 hours.

Do not give paracetamol with other medicines that contain paracetamol, such as some cold and flu remedies.

When to get help

  • Call 999 or go to A&E if you think your child has taken too much paracetamol, even if they seem well.
  • Speak to a doctor if your child still has a fever after 3 days, or you are worried.

Update, 3 November 2026: the advice for babies aged 2 to 3 months now matches the vaccination guidance. Earlier versions of this page said to speak to a doctor for any baby under 3 months.
