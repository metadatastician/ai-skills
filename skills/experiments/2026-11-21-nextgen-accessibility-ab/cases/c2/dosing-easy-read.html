Giving paracetamol to your child

Easy Read. Made on 14 March 2026.

Paracetamol is a medicine. It can help when your child has a high temperature or is in pain.

Use the spoon or syringe that comes in the box.

If your baby is under 3 months old, talk to a doctor first.

Give your child 1 dose every 4 hours.

Look at the box to see how much to give.

If you are worried, talk to a doctor.
